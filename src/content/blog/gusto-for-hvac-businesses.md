## How Gusto Works for HVAC Companies
Gusto is designed to take the stress out of your everyday payroll & HR tasks. 

Whether it’s onboarding a new team member or making sure payroll is done correctly and on time, Gusto handles it all. 

Here are all of the best features it offers and why it’s a game-changer for HVAC companies:

‍

  1. **Employee Onboarding**

We all know the paperwork that comes with hiring someone new. With Gusto, you can forget about endless forms and paperwork. New employees can fill out everything online—tax forms, bank account details, contact info—you name it, Gusto’s got it covered. It’s easy, quick, and best of all, paper-free.

‍

  2. **Time Tracking and Integration**

Keeping track of who worked when can be a hassle, especially when you’ve got crews out in the field. With Gusto, employees can clock in and out from their phones or any web browser, and all that data syncs automatically with payroll. 

If you prefer a more traditional setup, Gusto even has a [Time Clock Kiosk software](https://gusto.com/product/time-tools/kiosk) that turns your tablets into a time clock where employees can clock in and out. 

  3. **Payroll Processing**

Let’s be honest, payroll is one of the most time-consuming tasks, especially when mistakes happen. Gusto simplifies this by automating everything. No more chasing timecards or worrying about miscalculations. Everything is synced automatically, making payroll a breeze.

‍

  4. **Tax Compliance**

Nobody likes dealing with taxes, especially when they vary from state to state. Luckily, Gusto has you covered. It automatically calculates, files, and pays your local, state, and federal payroll taxes. Plus, it supports tax registration for all 50 states, so you stay compliant wherever you do business.

‍

  5. **Setting Up**

When it comes to setting up Gusto whether from scratch or switching from another payroll provider, the entire process is designed to be quick and easy. 

‍

Simply create an account and add your employee details or have them self-onboard.  Once Gusto has your employee and tax info, you can already run your first payroll in just a few clicks.

You can also switch over at any time of the year and Gusto will take care of importing all your previous payroll information, making the transition smooth and stress-free.

  2. **Job & Workforce Costing: **HVAC jobs vary in size and complexity, and Gusto makes it easy to track labor costs for each job. You can break down costs by project or task, giving you insights into employee efficiency and helping you create detailed invoices for your clients.

  4. **Next-Day Direct Deposits:  **Save yourself from the need to manage multiple apps and payment methods like wire transfers, ACH payments, and others. Gusto’s next-day direct deposit feature streamlines all these processes into one platform.

  5. **Automatic Tax Filing:**  Tax compliance can be a nightmare, especially if you operate across multiple states. Gusto takes care of calculating, filing, and paying your local, state, and federal taxes, so you can stay compliant without lifting a finger.
