## What’s a KPI Dashboard, and Why Does It Matter?
[Our KPI dashboard](https://myatlasaccountant.mykajabi.com/offers/vA2oktX3/checkout) at **Atlas Accounting Group**  is designed to make business tracking simple and effective. It’s divided into two main parts:

  * **KPI Dashboard  **– **Company Objective:  **This tracks your progress over time so you can see what’s working.

Let’s break it down further.

### KPI Drivers – Aligning Actions with Results
Think of this section as your business roadmap. It starts with setting clear goals for your company, then walks you through how to break those goals down into specific action steps. 

You decide who’s responsible for each piece, how you’ll measure success, and what progress looks like in real terms.

**Here’s what it includes:**

  * **Goals:  **What you want to achieve.
  * **Action Items:  **The specific tasks that move you toward your goal.
  * **Key Influencers:  **The team members responsible for getting it done.
  * **Scoring Mechanism:**  A simple way to measure whether it’s working.
  * **Results Section:  **A place to track what kind of impact your efforts are having.

Instead of vague objectives or hopeful guessing, you have a clear structure that helps you track what’s working and what’s not. You’ll be able to spot who’s moving the needle and which areas might need extra attention. 

The best part? It creates accountability and clarity without extra meetings or busywork.

### KPI Dashboard – Tracking Performance Over Time
Once your goals are set, you need a way to measure progress. That’s the job of the KPI dashboard. It helps you:

  * Track financial and operational data over time.
  * Analyze how you’re doing month over month.
  * Compare quarterly and annual trends to fine-tune your strategy.
  * Assign data owners to make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

It’s designed to help you understand how you’re doing month over month, quarter over quarter, and even year over year. And we use actual dollar amounts, percentages, and raw numbers so you’re not relying on gut instinct alone. 

Instead of just looking backward, the dashboard helps you identify patterns and predict where things are headed. It’s not just about tracking—it’s about using the data to make smarter decisions moving forward.
