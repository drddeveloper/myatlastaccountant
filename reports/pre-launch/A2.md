## Recommendations not applied (R-1 … R-5)

### R-1 — PF-6: 62 over-length titles are **one** decision, not 62 edits (answers D-5)
Do not fix these page by page. `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro:81` renders `<title>{title} | {SITE_DATA.company.name}</title>`, appending a fixed **25-character** suffix `" | Atlas Accounting Group"` to every page.

Arithmetic, computed from the regenerated report (not estimated):

- 62 titles exceed 60 characters.
- **45 of the 62 would fall to ≤ 60 characters if that suffix were dropped entirely.**
